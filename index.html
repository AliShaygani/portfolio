Enclosure Wall Bracket

Overview:
Designed a sheet-metal wall-mount bracket that holds a 20 kg electrical enclosure 100 mm off the wall, made from a single laser-cut blank with two press-brake bends and no welds.
Responsibilities:
✅ Defined the requirements: 294 N design load per bracket (3× handling factor), FoS ≥ 2 on yield, ≤ 1.0 mm sag.
✅ Compared three concepts with hand calculations and a weighted decision matrix; selected a triangular web.
✅ Modeled the bracket in SolidWorks Sheet Metal and ran FEA in SolidWorks Simulation with a mesh convergence study.
✅ Iterated on thickness, web height and washers after FEA showed the bolted joint, not the arm, was the critical spot.
✅ Produced the manufacturing drawing (BRKT-001) with GD&T per ASME Y14.5-2018.
Tools & Methods:
🛠️ SolidWorks Sheet Metal | SolidWorks Simulation (FEA) | Hand Calculations | GD&T | DFM
Results & Impact:
🔩 Final design in 3 mm steel (0.78 kg): FoS 3.3, 0.40 mm deflection and a buckling factor of 26.7, meeting every strength and stiffness requirement.
